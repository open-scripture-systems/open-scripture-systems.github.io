Open Scripture Systems

We are Christian volunteers who help open Bible translations and study tools become carefully checked and easy to use, beginning with Japanese.

Open means anyone may read, copy and share them at no cost. We want them to be as trustworthy as they are free.

What we do

  • We build free study tools.Tools for reading the Bible beside the Greek and Hebrew, in Japanese and English, made only from open data.
  • We check open translations and data.Volunteers read them beside the Greek and write down what could be better.
  • We send what we find to the editors.Volunteers suggest. The editors of each translation decide what changes.

Our projects

  • Word of God WorkshopFree tools for studying the words of the Bible in Greek and Hebrew: a reader, word studies, and a study workspace in Japanese and English.
  • Freedom Bible ReviewThe Japanese Freedom Bible is a public domain translation that is still a draft. We read it beside the Greek and send suggestions to its publisher, eBible.org.
  • Japanese Strong's linksEach word of the Kougo-yaku New Testament linked to the Greek word behind it. A computer made the first draft, and it is marked as a draft until people have checked it.

Who decides

Volunteers check and suggest. They do not change any translation. The final decision stays with the editors of each translation. A small review committee is being formed to look at suggestions before they are sent.

Everyone who helps agrees to a short statement of faith. You can read it on the Join us page.

Open Scripture Systems is a volunteer group, not a company, and it is not affiliated with any Bible publisher or Bible software company. Every source and its licence is on the sources page.
