Openly licensed data

  • TAGNT, Translators Amalgamated Greek New Testament

    STEPBible.org, based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge. CC BY 4.0. Source.

    Greek words, lemmas, Strong's numbers, morphology, glosses. Used in Word Study, Word Wheel, Passage Workbench, Grammar Lens, Verse Finder, Factbook and Compare Translations.

  • TAHOT, Translators Amalgamated Hebrew Old Testament

    STEPBible.org, based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge. CC BY 4.0. Source.

    Hebrew words, lemmas, glosses, morphology. Used in Hebrew Word Study, Word Wheel and Grammar Lens. Its parsing codes are read with the OpenScriptures Hebrew morphology code table and STEPBible's own additions to it (TEHMC, CC BY 4.0).

  • TIPNR, Translators Individualised Proper Names with all References

    STEPBible.org, based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge. CC BY 4.0. Source.

    People, places and things, with their names and references. Used in Factbook, Verse Finder and Passage Companion.

  • Cross references

    OpenBible.info. CC BY 4.0. Source.

    Cross references ranked by reader votes. Used in Passage Companion.

  • unfoldingWord Literal Text and Simplified Text

    unfoldingWord. CC BY-SA 4.0. ULT source, UST source.

    English renderings aligned to the Greek. Used in Word Study, Word Wheel, Passage Workbench and Compare Translations.

  • unfoldingWord Translation Notes

    unfoldingWord. CC BY-SA 4.0. Source.

    Notes on hard phrases, written to help translators. Used in Passage Companion.

    Public domain. Source.

    English text and renderings aligned to the Greek and Hebrew. Used in every tool that shows English Scripture. The Interlinear Reader is built from these tables alone, with their Nestle base Greek and Westminster Leningrad Codex Hebrew.

  • Freedom Bible (Japanese), フリーダム・バイブル

    eBible.org. Public domain (a draft translation). Source.

  • Robinson-Pierpont Byzantine Greek New Testament

    Maurice A. Robinson and William G. Pierpont. Public domain. Source.

    The Greek text the Freedom Bible follows, with Strong's numbers and parsing. Used in Freedom Bible Review.

  • World English Bible

    eBible.org. Public domain. Source.

    An English comparison text. Used in Freedom Bible Review.

  • Japanese Colloquial Bible (口語訳, 1955)

    Public domain in Japan; open question in the United States (see below). Source. The Japan Bible Society on its copyright.

    A Japanese comparison text, used in Freedom Bible Review and as the Japanese reading text of the Study Workspace. Shown as it is, with nothing changed. The file is titled the 1954/1955 edition and reads as the original wording, but it has not yet been compared line by line with the Japan Bible Society's list of later corrections.

    Public domain in Japan: the Japan Bible Society states that the copyright term of the 1955 edition has ended. In the United States it may still be protected until about 2049 (New Testament) and 2050 (Old Testament), under the rule that restored copyright in works from other countries. The Japan Bible Society would hold any such right. A request to remove this text from Japanese Wikisource for that reason has been open since 2023 and is not decided. If the rights holder asks, this site will remove the text. This is not legal advice.

  • Denmo Bible (電網聖書, 2002 draft)

    Public domain, by its makers' statement. Source.

    A modern Japanese translation made from the World English Bible. A draft of 13 books: Matthew to Acts, and Hebrews to Jude. Used in the Study Workspace.

What was changed

The data was reshaped to build these tools. Renderings are grouped by their main English word, translation notes were lightly cleaned of formatting, and counts were computed from the tagged words. The parts of this site derived from the unfoldingWord texts and notes are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Greek counts use the words STEPBible's TAGNT marks as NA28 readings. The Nestle-Aland edition's publisher did not provide or review this data. Mistakes in how the data is combined here are this site's own.

What is not used

Copyrighted translations such as the ESV and NIV are not used. No data, text or interface from any commercial Bible software is used. These tools are a study aid, not a replacement for a full Bible software library.

Privacy

No accounts and no analytics. Your browser remembers the last word or passage you opened, your display choices, and whether you turned on Larger text, on your device only. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google can see that request; if they are blocked, the pages still work with your device's fonts. The Feedback button sends your note to a Google Form, so Google receives it; the note carries the page name and its language, and an email address only if you write one. Notes are read privately and are not published.

Your browser also remembers, on your device only, whether you closed the updates notice.
